The method

From a single goal down to a task you can measure.

Everything starts with your purpose and cascades down until it becomes a task that is simply done, or not yet. Each layer runs through its own improvement loop. Build it this way, and the only direction is up.

The goal-to-task pyramid

Purpose to Objectives to Projects to measurable Tasks. Nothing floats; every task ladders up to the goal.

The improvement loop

Every layer of the pyramid runs this cycle. Each pass makes the system a little better.

What makes a task measurable

This is the smallest unit of the system. Clear inputs, a clear output, an owner, and a KPI, so “done” is never a debate.

Build a business this way and the only path is to grow.
